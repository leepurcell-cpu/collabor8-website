01Chapter 01: What we do

What we do

Three ways we help large businesses grow across borders.

Deploy

AI agents change how work gets done, so they need roles, rules and clear lines of approval. We design that operating model around how your business really runs, then work with specialist engineers to build, test and deploy the agents.

Explore AI agents

Enter

Businesses from outside the region face a long list of decisions. We test the market and your proposition, then set the order: onshore or free zone, licensing, core team and local partners. We manage delivery, so the first year is not spent fixing the first month.

Grow

Growth rarely comes from going it alone. We find the companies, partners and options that move a business forward, then bring the right people to the table. We test the fit, broker the introduction and structure the terms so both sides want to keep the deal.

AI agents, designed around your business

Many AI pilots impress in a demo and stall in the business. We start with how the business actually runs: its processes, roles and rules. We design agents to fit, set who approves and who audits, and work with specialist engineers to build and deploy them. Your people stay in charge.

  1. Map: find where agents can safely take on work.

  2. Design: define each agent's role, rules and approvals.

  3. Build: engineer and test with our technology partner.

  4. Govern: keep control as agents spread across the group.

  5. Embed: train teams so they own the result.

03Chapter 03: Selected work

Selected work

Decisions like yours, and how they turned out.

Entering the Gulf

International and large domestic businesses entered the UAE and Gulf with our support. We advised on strategy and regulatory structure, secured the required approvals and managed execution end to end.

Growing across Africa

A leading African fintech expanded across several markets through partnerships with major regional players. We structured the deals and scaled lending products into new territories.

Turning a business around

A regional operation of more than 120 people was restructured around clearer segments and a sharper proposition. The plan was built with the team who had to run it. Attrition fell from over 40% to a single departure in a year.
