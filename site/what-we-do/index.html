Be operating in a new market, not still researching it.

Right whenYou are entering a market you do not yet know well.

Licences, people and local partners lined up in the right order, so you open with confidence instead of surprises.

The situation

A new market looks simple from a distance. Up close it is regulation, licensing, hiring and local partners, each depending on the others. Get the order wrong and months disappear.

How we get you there

  1. Read the market properly. Regulation, competition and the partners worth talking to.
  2. Plan the way in. Structure, licensing and first hires, in the right sequence.
  3. Open with the right partners in place, and stay alongside you through the early months.

Have the right partner on the right terms, not a deal that stalls.

Right whenA partner could change your growth and the terms are not settled.

From first introduction to a signed agreement both sides can live with.

The situation

A good partnership can change your growth. A badly structured one costs more than having none. Most deals stall on roles, risk and money, not on intent.

How we get you there

  1. Find the right partner. Who fits, and who adds something you cannot build yourself.
  2. Structure the deal. Roles, risk and commercial terms set out clearly before anyone signs.
  3. Carry it through to signature, and set it up so it works in practice.

Run the business you have now, not the one you started with.

Right whenThe way you started no longer fits the size you have become.

Structure, roles and ways of working that fit where you are now.

The situation

What worked at ten people strains at fifty and breaks at two hundred. The signs are familiar: decisions slow down, roles overlap and the same few people sit in every decision.

How we get you there

  1. See how the business really runs, not how the chart says it does.
  2. Redesign what is holding it back. Structure, roles and decision rights.
  3. Make it stick. Move the people and the routines with the design.

Have a leadership team that carries the business, not one that depends on a few people.

Right whenThe business depends on a leadership team that is not yet in place.

The right people in the right seats, and the support to grow them.

The situation

A business can only grow as far as its leadership team can carry it. Gaps show up late, as missed targets and tired senior people, long after they could have been fixed cheaply.

How we get you there

  1. Define what the next stage needs from the people leading it.
  2. Find, appoint or develop the people to deliver it.
  3. Back them through the first year, when new leadership is most tested.

Put technology and AI where they make a real difference, not where the hype points.

Right whenYou are weighing where technology and AI belong in the business.

Practical choices, tested against your business, not the hype.

The situation

Every supplier says their technology will transform your business. Few say where it will not help. The real cost is rarely the licence. It is the time and attention spent on the wrong thing.

How we get you there

  1. Start with the work, not the tool. Where time and money are actually spent.
  2. Test what is worth doing. Practical pilots with clear measures before any large commitment.
  3. Put it to work in the business, with the people who will use it.

Find your next stage of growth, not wait for it to find you.

Right whenYou know you want to grow, and not yet where.

Where to play, and how to get there.

The situation

Most businesses know they want to grow. Fewer know which of the options in front of them is worth the effort. Choosing badly costs time you do not get back.

How we get you there

  1. Map the real options. New customers, new markets, new products, new partners.
  2. Weigh them honestly. Return, risk and what each asks of your people.
  3. Commit to one plan, with clear first steps and owners.
