AI agents

AI agents that work inside your business

We design how AI agents work in complex organisations: their roles, rules and approvals. Specialist engineers build them. People stay in charge.

Book a call

Many AI pilots impress in a demo and stall in the business. The cause is rarely the technology. It is unclear roles, missing rules and teams who were not asked. We start with how the business actually runs, then design agents to fit it.

How we deploy agents

  1. Map

    We study your processes, roles and decision points, and find where agents can take on work safely. We recommend which tasks to hand over first and which to leave with people.

  2. Design

    We define each agent's role, its rules and the point where a person approves. Designs reflect how work really flows across countries, functions and business units, not how the organisation chart says it should.

  3. Build

    Our technology partner engineers and tests the agents with your teams. They can run where suits you: public cloud, a controlled setup, or fully inside your own environment. [CHECK: deployment options the partner supports]

  4. Govern

    We set who approves, who audits and how control is kept as agents spread across a group. Every action can be traced to an agent and a policy. [CHECK: traceability claim]

  5. Embed

    Agents last only if people own them. We train teams, adjust roles and involve the people who run each process from day one, so the change is theirs.

Strategy and technology, together

Most AI work fails from one side. Some firms build well but do not know the business. Some advisers know the business but cannot build. Collabor8 pairs senior business advice with deep engineering. One side judges the business, the process and the risk. The other builds and runs the technology. You get one team.

Typical engagement

It starts with an informal 30 minute virtual coffee, to talk through what you are trying to achieve and whether we can help. If there is a fit, we hold a working session of two to three hours to map processes and roles, shaped around your business. Then a design phase and a pilot of one or two agents in a real process. Governance is built in from the start. Scope, fees and timing are agreed in writing before work begins.
